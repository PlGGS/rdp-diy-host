rdp.diy Host

Lets this Mac be controlled from the rdp.diy web console. It runs in the background with no window, Dock icon or menu bar item.

After installing, macOS asks you to allow Screen Recording and Accessibility for rdp.diy Host. Both are needed to see and control this Mac.
